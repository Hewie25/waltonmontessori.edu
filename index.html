Our Montessori Approach

At Walton Montessori we follow the principles pioneered by Dr Maria Montessori. Children learn through purposeful activity in a carefully prepared environment that respects their natural development.

  • Prepared environment that invites exploration
  • Mixed-age classes that encourage peer learning
  • Independence and responsibility from an early age
  • Practical life skills alongside academic work
  • Respect for each child’s unique pace and interests
