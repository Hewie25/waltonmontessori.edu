Learn More About Us!


Founded back in 2017, Walton started off as a small time basic school, and has since grown beautifully under the guidance and management of its administration.

The school has been able to send of 5 batches of its students into Secondary School, with over 65% of these students making it into their first-choice, and 24% making it into their second-choice.

Even today, the school works its hardest to instill good principles and morals, such as Hardwork, Patience, Kindness, and Sharing, so they not only fit into the best part of society, but they grow to make these principles a part of who they are.

The teachers are no exception to this motive, has they do their hardest best, with dedication, to ensure our lovely students are cared for, listened to, guided, and well taught, making it easy for them to feel welcomed and belong.

The growth and safety of your child, to us, is our sole duty and we take full responsibility of them once you leave them in our care.
