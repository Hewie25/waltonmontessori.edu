Learn More About Us

Building the next generation with care, character and excellence

Founded in 2017, Walton Montessori School began as a small basic school and has grown steadily under dedicated leadership. We have successfully sent five cohorts of students into secondary school, with over 65 % gaining admission to their first-choice schools and a further 24 % to their second-choice schools.

Today we continue to place strong emphasis on character. Hard work, patience, kindness and sharing are woven into daily life so that these values become part of who our pupils are—not just rules they follow.

Our teachers share the same commitment. With dedication and care they listen to, guide and teach every child, helping each one feel welcomed and valued. The growth, safety and well-being of your child is our primary responsibility the moment they are in our care.
